## 🔬 Idea 01 — Compiler Loop Optimization

### Research Question

How can modern compilers improve loop optimization to achieve better performance on different types of workloads?

### Why I Find It Interesting

Loops are executed repeatedly and can have a major impact on program performance. Modern processors are also becoming increasingly parallel and complex.

This makes me interested in understanding how compilers such as GCC and LLVM automatically optimize loops.

### What I Know So Far

- Compilers transform source code into more efficient machine code.
- Loops are important targets for optimization.
- GCC and LLVM provide multiple optimization techniques.
- Optimization can reduce execution time and improve hardware utilization.

### What I Want to Explore

- How GCC and LLVM decide which loop optimizations to apply.
- The effect of different compiler optimization levels.
- Performance differences before and after optimization.
- How optimization behaves for different workloads.
